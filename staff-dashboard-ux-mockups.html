4 · The token fragmentation, made visible

Four "muted text" tokens exist in app/globals.css; these three are actually in use across the pages audited here.

text on Live Scenarios
--text-muted #577067
text on Modules
--color-text-muted #6b7280
adjacent, unused here
--color-text-subtle #737373

Three "status color" systems live in this same 3-page flow — not wrong individually, but crossing all three in one session is what makes the pages feel unrelated.
