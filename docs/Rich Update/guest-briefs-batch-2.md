# Live Guest briefs, batch 2: on the floor

11 guests. Each one is a short typed conversation of 3 to 6 replies. The staff member sees the scene and the guest's first line. The AI plays the guest from the rest of the brief, which the staff member only sees in the reveal afterwards, along with what the guest was thinking at each turn.

A conversation passes at 75 out of 100. Breaking the rule that can't be broken ends it straight away and is never a pass.

**The four dials** show in the reveal: the guest's mood, and speed, safety and money when the situation puts them at stake. Money counts anything given away or made again at cost, and an extra sale at menu price. The figures are rough, and each thing counts once.

**How patient the guest is** follows the person's progress. A beginner gets a patient guest (starts one mood better, forgives a slip). Once the module is mastered the guest plays as written. Once the module is mastered and the guest passed before, or 10 modules are mastered, the guest is tough (starts one mood worse, pushes back once, brings the twist in early).

## Let me help you, love (Helen, module 5)

*gentle, starts happy. From: AI Arena, module 5. Id: `helpful-plates`. Not live yet.*

**Staff see before they start:**

- The scene: Sunday lunch, a busy bistro. You're clearing a big table onto your tray: heavy schooner glasses, wine glasses and a half-full water jug. The tray is already at a safe load. Helen, one of the guests, picks up a wobbly stack of plates to hand you.
- Helen opens with: "Here love, take these plates too, save you a trip."

**Hidden until the reveal:**

- **Who:** Helen, in her 70s, a warm grandmother who likes to help. She means well and is a little hurt if she's brushed off, but laughs easily.
- **Wants:** To be helpful, and to feel appreciated for it.
- **Calms them:**
  - A warm thank you
  - Politely saying no to the plates for now, and why: the tray is full
  - Saying you'll come straight back for the plates
  - A bit of light humour
  - Keeping the load steady while you talk
- **Sets them off:**
  - Snapping "Don't touch that"
  - Taking the plates and wobbling the tray
  - Ignoring her
  - Making her feel silly for trying
- **Twist (2nd or 3rd reply):** She starts putting the plates onto the edge of your tray anyway, saying it's no trouble.
- **Rule that can't be broken:** none
- **A good ending:** The tray stays safe with only drinks on it, the plates wait for your next trip, and Helen feels thanked rather than told off.

**The dials:**

- Guest: always in play (the mood).
- Speed: not in play.
- Safety: in play, starts at "Watch it".
- Money: not in play.
