## Points to check

This batch has more points than the others, because RSA and food safety rules differ by state. None of the new questions tests a doubtful part. These existing statements are already live in the quiz, and I'd fix them:

**RSA Compliance**
- **Minors and parents (statement 3):** "A minor can legally drink alcohol if their parent buys it" is false. The explanation says this carries big fines. Rules differ by state: some allow a parent to supply their own child in limited cases, and most venues refuse anyway. I'd reword it to: "Staff can serve a minor alcohol on licensed premises if a parent orders it for them" (false), explained as "In most states this is an offence, and venue policy is to refuse. Check your state's rules."
- **Accepted ID (statement 8):** the explanation lists "Driver's Licence, Passport, Keypass". Keypass is Victoria only, and each state has its own proof-of-age card and accepts digital licences. I'd change it to "government-issued photo ID with a date of birth, such as a driver's licence, passport or your state's proof-of-age card". Q7 already uses that wording.
- **Free water (statement 4):** this is right across the states. No change.

**Food Safety**
- **Defrosting prawns (statement 6):** the explanation says to thaw under running cold water. Food authorities name the fridge first, with cold running water or the microwave as other options. I'd change it to "Thaw in the fridge, or under cold running water if you need it sooner." No question asks about this.
- **Blue band-aids (statement 8):** the statement says they "must" be worn. The law requires a covered wound; blue is industry practice. I'd change "must" to "should". Q8 asks only why they're blue.
- **Danger zone (5°C to 60°C):** this is the national standard, so Q3 and Q4 ask for the two numbers. This is the one place I've used numbers in this batch, because they're national.

**Conflict De-escalation**
- **"Two-arm-length distance" (statement 3):** a trainer's rule of thumb, not a standard. Q3 says "a safe distance". I'd drop the number from the explanation.

**Evacuation Protocols**
- **Checking bathrooms (statement 5):** good practice, but only if it's safe to do so and the warden directs it. I'd add that to the explanation. No question asks about it.
- **Fire warden in charge (statement 4):** right, until the fire brigade arrives and takes over. I'd add that.

**Opening & Closing**
- **"Counting the till float exactly is the first opening duty" (statement 1):** the order of opening tasks is venue policy. I'd reword it to "The till float must be counted and checked at opening" (true). No question asks which task comes first.
- **"You can leave the coffee machine on overnight" (statement 2, false):** many commercial machines are designed to stay on, or run on a timer, and some manufacturers recommend it. This one could be wrong for some venues. I'd reword it to "Follow your venue's rule for the coffee machine overnight; never leave it on if the venue switches it off." Or drop it. No question uses it.
- **Glass bins at 2 AM (statement 7):** the real rule is usually a condition on the venue's licence or a council noise rule, and the hours vary. I'd change the explanation to "Check your venue's licence conditions: most limit bottle bin emptying late at night."

**Inventory Control**
- **"Free-pouring is ... often illegal" (statement 7):** free-pouring isn't illegal. It's against most venues' policy. I'd change "illegal" to "against venue policy". No question uses it.
- **"Unauthorised comps are legally considered theft" (statement 2):** strong, but fair. Giving away stock without approval is theft from the employer. No change.
