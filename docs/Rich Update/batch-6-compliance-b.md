## Points to check

**Existing statements I'd fix.** None of the new questions tests the doubtful part:

- **Jigger Precision: "The standard single spirit measure in an Australian venue is 30ml"**, explained as "the legal standard. Venues that use a different measure must clearly display that fact." 30 ml is the usual measure, but I can't find a national law that makes it the legal standard or requires a sign. I'd change the explanation to "30ml is the standard measure in most Australian venues. Use your venue's jigger for every pour." Q1 asks "in most Australian venues", using that wording.

The real nightly job is backflushing and cleaning the group heads.
