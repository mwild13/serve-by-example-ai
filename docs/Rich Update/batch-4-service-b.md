- **"Sorry, we're out of that." Then walk away (right)**

1. Acknowledge that it's unavailable

- **Deep menu knowledge (right)**
- A script from the manager

Explanation: You cannot pivot to an alternative if you do not know what is comparable on the menu. Product knowledge is the foundation.

- **Once, briefly (right)**

## Points to check

**Statements that may be wrong or vary by venue.** No new question tests the doubtful part of any of these:

- **Docket Reading: "M/R... is a universal POS abbreviation"** and **"G/F universally stands for Gluten Free".** These are common, but POS abbreviations vary by venue and system. Q1 says "usually". I'd change "universal" to "common" in both explanations.
- **The Swivel Head: "Scanning the room every 60 seconds".** A number a venue might set differently. No question asks for it. I'd reword it to "Scanning the room regularly helps prevent guests from feeling ignored."
- **Natural Upselling: "The 'Power of Three' means suggesting a specific premium brand".** In sales training, "power of three" usually means offering three choices. The statement's lesson (name a specific product) is right, but the name may confuse staff. I'd rename it "naming the product".
- **Natural Upselling, statement 2:** the explanation says pushy upselling "destroys trust, tips, and any chance of return visits". I'd drop "tips" for the same reason we did in Suggestive Selling.
- **Clearing Dead Soldiers: "Removing a dead soldier without first asking the guest is rude"** (false). Some higher-end venues teach a quick "May I take this?". Q3 follows the statement as written. Tell me if your venues ask first.
